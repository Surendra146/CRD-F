Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

HanuRam Tech provides this CRM application and its WhatsApp integration. This policy explains how personal information is handled through its customer relationship management (CRM) service and WhatsApp integration. It covers account holders, business contacts, customers whose records are stored in the CRM, and people who communicate with a business using the service.

Who is responsible for your information

The business that collects your information and uses HanuRam Tech determines why its customer records and communications are processed. HanuRam Tech processes those records to provide the CRM service for that business. For questions about a particular business's records, contact that business directly. Account and service administration data is used to operate HanuRam Tech.

Information processed

  • Customer and CRM records: names, email addresses, phone numbers (including WhatsApp numbers), business details, tags, customer preferences, marketing opt-in information, purchase or sales records, and other information entered or imported by authorized users.
  • Communications: message content, templates, attachments where used, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, campaign information, and delivery or read status. These records may be processed when the relevant messaging features are enabled.
  • Account and technical information: account details, roles and permissions, authentication information, and technical logs such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and errors generated while operating or securing the service.

Information comes from you, the business you interact with, authorized CRM users, uploaded records, and connected services such as WhatsApp. Businesses should only import information they are entitled to process.

Why information is processed

Information is used to maintain customer records, provide customer support, manage sales and customer relationships, send requested service updates, organize campaigns, report on communications, administer accounts, and investigate errors or abuse. Marketing communications should be sent only with the required permission or another applicable legal basis. Businesses are responsible for honoring consent, preferences, and opt-out requests.

WhatsApp integration and Meta processing

When a business enables the WhatsApp Business Platform integration, recipient phone numbers, message content, templates, media where applicable, and messaging metadata are transmitted to WhatsApp/Meta to deliver business communications. WhatsApp/Meta may return messages and delivery or read events to the business's configured webhook, where receiving features are enabled.

The business and its authorized service providers can access the business communications they process. Meta/WhatsApp operates its infrastructure and processes information under its applicable terms and policies. Do not assume that a message to a business is handled in the same way as a private conversation between individuals. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy and WhatsApp Business Data Processing Terms.

Sharing and service providers

Records are accessible to authorized users of the relevant business and to service providers needed to operate the service, such as hosting, database, and messaging providers. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims. Providers may process information in countries other than your own; the business must consider applicable transfer requirements when configuring its services.

Retention and security

Customer records and communication history are retained while needed for the business's customer relationship, service delivery, and applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the business's settings and requirements; there is no universal fixed retention period for all records. Records should be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed. Backups and technical logs may remain until their normal retention cycles expire, and some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or disputes.

The service uses account authentication and permissions to limit access. Production deployments should use HTTPS and keep messaging credentials on the backend rather than exposing them in the browser. Businesses and operators are responsible for securing their hosting, accounts, access credentials, and backups. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Browser storage

The CRM uses browser storage to maintain sign-in state and service preferences. This privacy policy is publicly accessible without signing in or providing customer information. Hosting providers may generate ordinary access logs when you visit.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask the business holding your records for access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your information, and withdraw marketing permission. Applicable rights depend on your location and circumstances. To stop WhatsApp marketing, reply with an opt-out request, such as “STOP,” to the business sending the messages. The business must review and honor that request; this policy does not imply automatic opt-out processing.

For deletion, identify the business and the phone number or email associated with your records, and describe which information you want removed. The recipient may request proportionate verification to prevent unauthorized deletion. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary identity documents. Requests are reviewed and handled under applicable requirements; information subject to a legal retention requirement may be retained, with the reason explained. Deleting CRM records does not automatically delete copies held independently by Meta/WhatsApp or other recipients.

Contact and privacy requests

For records or WhatsApp messages from a particular business, contact that business using the contact details it provided, or reply to its existing WhatsApp conversation with “Privacy request” or “Delete my data.” This lets the business locate and review your request.

For questions about HanuRam Tech, account information, or assistance with access, correction, and deletion requests, contact HanuRam Tech at hanuramtech@gmail.com. Use the subject “Privacy request” or “Data deletion request.” If your request concerns a customer record held for another business, include that business's name so we can coordinate with the responsible organization.

Policy updates

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. The business should communicate material changes when required.
